Version control is important in collaborative projects because it allows multiple people to work on the same codebase without overwriting each other's work. 
It keeps a complete history of changes, so teams can see who made what change, when, and why. 
If a mistake is introduced, version control makes it possible to roll back to a previous working version instead of losing progress. 
It also supports parallel development through branching, letting team members experiment safely before merging into the main project. 
Overall, version control improves accountability, collaboration, and reliability.
